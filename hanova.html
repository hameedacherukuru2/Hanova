Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement 26136

Give good ideas a fair, safe way into government.

Government departments rarely hear about the startups with the right answers. HANOVA is the missing pathway: post the real problem, match it to eligible startups, run a small controlled trial, and only then decide — with a person signing off at every gate.

How it works

One pathway, six accountable steps

Nothing skips ahead. Each step hands off cleanly to the next, and a person signs off before it moves.

1
Challenge intake

The department names the real problem

Not a vague brief — the outcome it wants, its constraints, and exactly how success will be measured.

Outcome templatesConstraintsSuccess metrics
2
Discover & evaluate

Eligible startups are found and scored

The pool is checked against eligibility rules, independent experts are matched in, and every score is recorded transparently.

Startup discoveryEligibility checksExpert matching
3
Controlled pilot

A small, sandboxed trial runs first

The startup delivers against clear milestones in a bounded pilot — nothing touches production or real budgets yet.

SandboxMilestonesNo live risk
4
Evidence & decision

An independent reviewer validates the results

Milestone evidence is checked by someone who wasn't running the pilot, before any scale-up conversation starts.

Independent validationAudit trail
5
Procurement / scale

An authorised official makes the call

If the evidence holds up, the department moves to procurement through its existing, statutory process — HANOVA hands off, it doesn't decide.

Human sign-offExisting procurement rules
🔒
The rule that never bends: human-in-the-loop, rule-based eligibility, a full audit trail, milestone evidence — and no automated procurement decision, ever.
